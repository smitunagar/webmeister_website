Research-led impact

Built for responsible kitchens, not wasteful systems.

IOMS supports the full food-waste hierarchy: prevent first, identify eligible surplus for responsible redirection, and measure the environmental and social impact of better decisions.

Prevent avoidable waste

AI vision, weighing and forecasting help teams identify recurring waste patterns before overproduction or over-ordering becomes routine.

Surface eligible surplus

Clear surplus visibility can support responsible workflows for redirecting suitable food to food banks and charitable organisations.

Reduce carbon impact

Reducing unused food also reduces the emissions tied to producing, transporting, storing and disposing of food that never gets eaten.

Support food access

Better operational data can contribute to the broader social objective of improving food access for vulnerable communities.

This work is guided by university-backed experimentation, advisor input, sustainability research and real kitchen data. IOMS is designed as an applied research platform for more responsible food operations.
